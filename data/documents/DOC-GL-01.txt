Guideline 'Hypertension Confirmation Guideline': Confirm elevated readings on repeated measurements before starting long-term therapy. It is associated with Hypertension and requires the Blood Pressure Measurement. Guideline 'ACE-Inhibitor-Like Renal Monitoring Guideline': Check kidney function before and after starting Cardivex. It is associated with Cardivex and requires the Serum Creatinine Test.
