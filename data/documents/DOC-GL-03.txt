Guideline 'Asthma Lung Function Guideline': Document baseline airflow limitation when asthma is suspected. It is associated with Asthma and requires the Spirometry. Guideline 'Heart Failure Imaging Guideline': Assess cardiac structure and function when heart failure is suspected. It is associated with Heart Failure and requires the Echocardiogram.
