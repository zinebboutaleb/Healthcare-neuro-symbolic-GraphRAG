title: Overview of Osteoarthritis
doc_type: disease_overview
---
Osteoarthritis is a fictional teaching entry describing degeneration of joint cartilage. Reported symptoms include Joint Pain and Joint Stiffness. Listed risk factors are Obesity and Advanced Age. The condition is assessed using Joint X-Ray. Management options listed in this knowledge base are Arthrocalm, Physical Therapy and Weight Management Program.
