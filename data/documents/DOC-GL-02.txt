---
Guideline 'Diabetes Monitoring Guideline': Track long-term glucose control at regular intervals. It is associated with Type 2 Diabetes and requires the HbA1c Test. Guideline 'Insulin-Secretagogue Review Guideline': Review glucose control when Sulfaglide is started or its dose is changed. It is associated with Sulfaglide and requires the HbA1c Test.
